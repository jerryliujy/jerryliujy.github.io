ABOUT

Hi, I’m Jingyuan.

Hi there! I’m Jingyuan, currently an undergraduate student at Fudan University. I work on vision-language-action models, reward modeling and reinforcement learning for robotics, world models for manipulation, and continual, self-improving robot learning. My long-term vision is to develop general-purpose and trustworthy robots that can perform a wide range of everyday tasks, continually adapt in new environments, and operate reliably and safely in the real world.

Outside of research, I’m also a big fan of football and baseball!
