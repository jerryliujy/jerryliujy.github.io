Robotics & Embodied AI

Hi there! I’m Jingyuan, currently an undergraduate student at Fudan University. My research interests center around building general-purpose and trustworthy robotic systems. My long-term vision is to develop robots that can 1) perform a wide range of everyday tasks, 2) continually adapt and improve in new environments, and 3) operate reliably and safely in the real world.

Outside of research, I’m also a big fan of football and baseball!
